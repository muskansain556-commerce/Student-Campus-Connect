ABOUT THE PROJECT

A connected campus
starts with clear information.

Campus Connect is a student-focused website concept that brings common college information together in a clean and accessible layout.

Problem statement

Students may need to check several sources for notices, study links, events and contact information. When information is scattered, it can take extra time to find the right details.

Our solution

This website places key student resources in one organised digital space, with clear navigation, responsive pages and a simple feedback form.

Project objectives

  • Organise student information
  • Provide simple page navigation
  • Share learning resources and events
  • Encourage student feedback
  • Work across mobile and desktop screens

WHO IS IT FOR?

Designed for the campus community

Students

Find study links, events and essential information quickly.

Student clubs

Promote activities and help more students participate.

College support teams

Share updates and collect feedback in an organised way.
